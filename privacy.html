Who we are

Craftlane (“we”, “us”) operates craftlane.app and sells indie digital products for developers. Contact: admin@craftlane.app.

What we collect

  • Site: basic server / CDN logs (IP, user agent, request path) as provided by our hosting (e.g. Cloudflare).
  • Purchases: payment and delivery data handled by our checkout provider (Creem). We may receive your email and order details needed to fulfill downloads and support.
  • Email: if you write to us, we keep the correspondence as needed to respond.

How we use information

To operate the site, fulfill orders, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Processors

Hosting/CDN and payment processors act as processors or independent controllers for their services. Review Creem’s and Cloudflare’s privacy notices for how they handle data.

Retention

We keep order and support records as long as needed for fulfillment, tax, and dispute resolution, then delete or anonymize when no longer required.

Your choices

Email admin@craftlane.app to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. You may also manage preferences with our payment provider for purchase-related data.

Changes

We may update this page. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected here before or when they take effect.
